Account and Data Deletion Request

How to request deletion of your DASHED account.

If you have a DASHED account, you can request account deletion and deletion of associated personal data without accessing or reinstalling the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Email dasheddeliverycompany@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line DASHED Account and Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the phone number or email address associated with your account and state that you request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
  4. Do not send your password, mobile-money PIN, verification code or other security credentials.

What happens next?

DASHED will review the request and may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity. Once verified, the account and associated personal data that can be deleted will be deleted, subject to applicable legal, security, fraud-prevention, transaction-record and dispute-resolution requirements. If certain information must be retained, it will be limited to the relevant purpose and retained as required or permitted by law.

Sending a request does not itself mean deletion is complete. DASHED must verify and process the request.
